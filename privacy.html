政策摘要

本应用用于创建和管理报价单、维护业务资料并导出文件。当前本地版的报价及资料主要保存在你的设备上，不要求注册账号，不提供已启用的 AI 云端处理或应用内云同步，也不集成广告或第三方行为追踪服务。你主动分享的文件、Apple 内购，以及访问本政策网页涉及的外部服务，分别按下文说明处理。

1. 适用范围与责任主体

本政策说明 【开发者真实姓名】（以下简称“我们”）在提供 Quote Assistant 时如何处理信息。它适用于当前本地版应用及与我们的直接隐私联系。你主动打开的第三方网站、Apple 服务及接收导出文件的其他应用，也受其各自的隐私政策约束。

2. 应用在设备上处理的信息

根据你实际使用的功能，应用可能在设备上保存以下由你输入或选择的信息：

  • 报价单：编号、日期、客户资料、商品或服务明细、价格、税费、折扣、付款条件、备注、状态及历史版本。
  • 资料库：客户姓名、公司、联系方式、地址、商品资料和可复用的报价条款。
  • 企业资料：企业名称、地址、联系方式、税务标识及你选择的标志、签名或其他品牌图片。
  • 偏好与功能状态：语言、主题、触觉反馈、导出设置及购买权益状态。

这些信息用于保存和编辑记录、计算报价金额、生成报价文件及恢复你的使用偏好。当前本地版不会将你的报价单、客户资料或企业资料自动上传到开发者服务器。

如你录入客户或其他人的个人信息，请确保有适当权限，并仅录入完成业务所需的内容。

3. 图片与设备权限

使用图片相关功能时，应用可通过系统选择器读取你主动选取的图片，用于相应的品牌资料或页面功能。选择图片不代表授权我们访问整个照片库。你可以拒绝或取消选择，并在系统设置中管理应用实际请求的权限；相应功能可能因此不可用。

当前本地版不为报价管理功能收集你的精确位置或设备通讯录。

4. 导出、保存与分享

应用可在设备上生成 PDF 或 Excel 文件，并在应用临时目录中准备分享副本。只有在你选择保存或分享目的地后，文件才会交给你指定的应用、文件存储服务或接收方。

导出内容可能包含客户联系方式、价格及其他业务信息，请在分享前检查。导出后另存的副本由你和相应服务管理，不会随应用内记录的删除而自动删除。

5. Apple 内购与系统服务

如果你使用已提供的应用内购买或恢复购买功能，付款和交易由 Apple 的 App Store 系统处理。应用通过 StoreKit 获取产品、交易及权益信息，以判断可用功能。我们不会通过应用获取你的完整银行卡号或 Apple 账号密码。

Apple 可能根据你的设备设置处理购买信息、系统诊断和设备备份。设备备份可能包含应用本地数据；本应用没有独立提供已启用的云同步服务，并不表示系统备份被关闭。相关处理请参阅 Apple 隐私政策及设备设置。

6. 登录、AI、云功能与反馈

本政策所覆盖的本地版默认不开放账号登录、AI 导入或应用内云服务，不会将报价内容发送至 AI 服务商进行处理。若未来开放这些功能，我们会在启用前说明相关信息类别、处理目的、接收方、保存安排及所需的授权。

当前版本的应用内反馈提交采用模拟实现，不会实际向开发者发送反馈或附件；请通过本政策所列邮箱联系我们。若你主动通过邮箱联系，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、消息及你附带的资料，用于回复、处理问题或隐私请求。请避免发送不必要的敏感信息。邮件服务商也会按其自身的服务和隐私规则处理通信信息。

7. 广告、追踪与政策网页

当前本地版不集成广告 SDK 或第三方行为统计 SDK，不进行跨应用广告追踪，也不会出售你的个人信息或将其分享用于跨场景定向广告。

8. 保存期限与删除

报价和业务资料通常保存在你的设备上，直到你删除相应记录、使用“设置 → 清除本地数据”，或通过系统删除应用及其数据。“清除本地数据”会删除本机报价、相关历史、资料库及企业品牌资料，并保留应用偏好；它不会删除你已导出至其他位置的文件、系统备份或 Apple 的交易记录，也不会取消订阅。

Policy summary

The app helps you create and manage quotations, maintain business records, and export documents. In the current local version, quotations and business records are primarily stored on your device. Registration is not required, AI cloud processing and in-app cloud sync are not enabled, and the app does not integrate advertising or third-party behavioral tracking services. Files you choose to share, Apple purchases, and external services involved in viewing this policy are described below.

1. Scope and responsible developer

This policy explains how 【开发者真实姓名】 (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when providing Quote Assistant. It covers the current local version and privacy communications sent directly to us. Third-party websites you open, Apple services, and other apps that receive your exports are also subject to their respective privacy policies.

2. Information processed on your device

Depending on the features you use, the app may store the following information that you enter or select:

  • Quotations: numbers, dates, customer details, product or service line items, prices, taxes, discounts, payment terms, notes, statuses, and historical versions.
  • Library records: customer names, companies, contact details, addresses, product information, and reusable quotation terms.
  • Business profile: business name, address, contact details, tax identifiers, and logos, signatures, or other branding images you select.
  • Preferences and feature status: language, theme, haptic feedback, export settings, and purchase entitlement status.

This information is used to save and edit records, calculate quotation amounts, generate documents, and restore your preferences. The current local version does not automatically upload your quotations, customer records, or business profile to developer-operated servers.

If you enter personal information about customers or others, ensure that you have appropriate authorization and include only what your business task requires.

3. Images and device permissions

For image-related features, the app can read images you explicitly select using the system picker for the relevant branding or page feature. Selecting an image does not authorize access to your entire photo library. You may decline or cancel selection and manage permissions actually requested by the app in system settings; the corresponding feature may then be unavailable.

The current local version does not collect your precise location or device contacts for quotation management.

4. Exporting, saving, and sharing

The app can generate PDF or Excel files on your device and prepare sharing copies in its temporary directory. Files are handed to an app, storage service, or recipient only after you choose a saving or sharing destination.

Exports may contain customer contact details, prices, and other business information. Review them before sharing. Copies saved elsewhere are managed by you and the relevant service and are not automatically deleted when you delete records in the app.

5. Apple purchases and system services

If you use available in-app purchase or restore-purchase features, Apple’s App Store handles payment and transactions. The app uses StoreKit product, transaction, and entitlement information to determine available features. We do not obtain your full payment card number or Apple Account password through the app.

Apple may process purchase information, system diagnostics, and device backups according to your settings. Backups may contain local app data. The absence of an independently enabled in-app cloud sync service does not mean system backups are disabled. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and your device settings for details.

6. Sign-in, AI, cloud features, and feedback

The local version covered by this policy does not enable account sign-in, AI import, or in-app cloud services by default and does not send quotation content to AI providers for processing. If these features become available, we will explain the relevant information categories, purposes, recipients, retention arrangements, and required authorization before enabling them.

In-app feedback submission in the current version is simulated and does not actually send feedback or attachments to the developer. Please contact us using the email address in this policy. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments to respond to questions, address problems, or handle privacy requests. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Email providers also process communications under their own service and privacy rules.

7. Advertising, tracking, and this policy page

The current local version does not integrate advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, track you across apps for advertising, or sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

This policy page does not use analytics scripts, advertising scripts, external fonts, or tracking cookies that we set. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes access-related information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. Website hosting is separate from local storage in the app. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details.

8. Retention and deletion

Quotations and business records generally remain on your device until you delete the relevant records, use Settings → Clear Local Data, or delete the app and its data through the system. Clear Local Data removes quotations, related history, library records, and business branding on that device while keeping app preferences. It does not delete files exported elsewhere, system backups, or Apple transaction records, and it does not cancel subscriptions.

Temporary export files may remain until the system clears temporary storage or app data is deleted. Manage backups and third-party copies through the respective services.

Support communications you send directly to us are kept only as needed to address the issue, subsequent disputes, or applicable legal obligations, and deleted when no longer needed. You may request deletion using the email address below.

You can view, update, or delete supported records in the app, export quotation files, and manage device permissions and backup settings. We cannot remotely access or restore quotation records stored only on your device. Exporting a quotation document is not equivalent to backing up the entire database.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, object to processing, obtain data portability, withdraw consent, or complain to a supervisory authority. For information we actually hold, contact our privacy email. We will verify and handle requests under applicable law and will not treat you unfairly for exercising your legal rights.

We do not currently upload local quotation records to developer-operated servers, domestically or abroad. Apple services, email services, GitHub Pages, or sharing destinations you choose may process information outside your country or region, as described in their respective disclosures.
